## Important strategy notes

- Google does not use the `meta keywords` tag, so keywords are placed in useful visible copy, page titles, headings, descriptions, alt text, links, and page architecture instead.
- Do not create dozens of near-identical city + device combinations such as `/gilbert/laptop-recycling`, `/mesa/laptop-recycling`, etc. unless there is genuinely unique local content. The current architecture keeps city intent and service intent separate, then cross-links them.
- Keep city pages genuinely useful and update them with real local reviews, project examples, collection-event details, or operating information as EVER grows.
- Do not target certifications or services EVER does not actually hold or provide.

## Post-launch ranking work

1. Verify `electronicrecycle.net` in Google Search Console.
2. Submit `https://electronicrecycle.net/sitemap.xml`.
3. Inspect and request indexing for the homepage, `/services`, `/areas`, the 9 service pages, and the 8 city pages.
4. Create/verify the Google Business Profile when eligible and keep service areas consistent with the website.
5. Build real Google reviews and local citations.
6. Earn relevant links from local businesses, chambers, property managers, IT providers, schools, recycling directories, and community organizations.
7. Review Search Console queries monthly and expand pages based on real impressions rather than keyword stuffing.
